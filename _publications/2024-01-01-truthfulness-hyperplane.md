**Junteng Liu**, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

*EMNLP 2024*

This paper discovers a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models, demonstrating that truthfulness information is encoded in a consistent direction across different models and tasks. This finding enables effective probing and steering of LLM truthfulness.
